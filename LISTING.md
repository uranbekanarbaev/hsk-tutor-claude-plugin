# HSK Tutor — production directory copy

## Name

HSK Tutor

## Short description

HSK preparation with simple explanations, examples, HSK vocabulary and HSK grammar in chat. Choose your level and take an HSK mock test on hsk-tutor.com.

## Full description

Prepare for Chinese HSK 3.0 with short, practical lessons in your language. HSK Tutor helps you study 10 vocabulary words at a time, understand grammar through clear examples, practise daily and review mistakes.

Just say “HSK Tutor” and choose what to study. You do not need to remember separate commands. Try “Teach me 10 HSK 3 words”, “Explain 要 with examples”, or “I want an HSK 5 mock exam”.

Looking for an HSK practice test? Open the exam page for your chosen level on [hsk-tutor.com](https://hsk-tutor.com/mock-exam?utm_source=claude). Exams take place on the website, not inside Claude. The plugin does not automatically start an attempt, transfer your account or provide an official HSK score.

Chat lessons are AI-authored learning practice, not certified official exam questions. Current HSK 3.0 guidance cites official announcements and distinguishes trial exams from the global rollout.
